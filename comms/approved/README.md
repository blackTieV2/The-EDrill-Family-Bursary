# Approved Communications

Nothing should be placed in this folder until the final pre-publication check is complete.

Before any communication is moved here, the Chairman/Committee/Communications check must confirm that the wording:

- contains no fundraising language;
- does not describe the Bursary as a charity, society, foundation, public fund, or ROS body;
- does not claim tax-free or non-taxable treatment;
- does not imply guaranteed entitlement;
- does not identify children, beneficiaries, schools, personal stories, or family circumstances without specific written consent.

Draft communications remain in `comms/drafts/` until that check is recorded.
