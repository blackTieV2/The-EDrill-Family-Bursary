## Draft LinkedIn Post

Energy Drilling is pleased to introduce The EDrill Family Bursary, a company-funded education support initiative for eligible families connected with our Thailand operations.

The initiative reflects a simple belief: the people who support our rigs and operations also support families, futures, and communities. By assisting with approved schooling costs for eligible children, the Bursary is intended to recognise that contribution in a practical and meaningful way.

The first phase will be administered through our Thailand operations, with a controlled application and review process led by Bangkok HR, Finance, and Operations.

We are proud to take this step and look forward to learning from the first award cycle as the programme develops.
