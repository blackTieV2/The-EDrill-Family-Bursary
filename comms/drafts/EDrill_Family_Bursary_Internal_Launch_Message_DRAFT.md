## Draft Message

Subject: Introduction of The EDrill Family Bursary

Dear colleagues,

We are pleased to introduce The EDrill Family Bursary, a company-funded education support initiative for eligible families connected with our Thailand operations.

The Bursary is intended to assist with approved schooling costs for eligible children, subject to the approved criteria, available bursary places, and annual review.

For the first award cycle, the Bursary is planned for Thailand operations only. The application process will be administered by Bangkok HR, with support from Finance and Operations.

Key points:

- The Bursary is discretionary and limited.
- Eligibility does not guarantee an award.
- Renewal is not automatic.
- Applications must be supported by the required documents.
- Payments will be made only through the approved Finance process.
- Personal information will be handled according to the approved privacy notice and records controls.

Further details, including eligibility criteria, required evidence, application dates, and the application form, will be provided by Bangkok HR.
