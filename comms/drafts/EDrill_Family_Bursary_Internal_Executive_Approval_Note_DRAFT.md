# The EDrill Family Bursary - Internal Executive Approval Note

Status: Draft for internal review only  
Version: 0.1  
Purpose: record executive approval to proceed with the Phase 1 company-funded Bursary launch, subject to final document, launch administration, agency acknowledgement where applicable, and communications checks.

## 1. Decision Requested

Approval is requested to proceed with finalising The EDrill Family Bursary as an internal company-funded discretionary education support scheme for eligible families connected with EDrill Thailand operations.

Approval is also requested to continue preparing the document pack, application process, and communications, subject to final review by Finance/Payroll, HR, Operations, the Committee, and Communications.

## 2. Proposed Launch Scope

| Item | Proposed position |
| --- | --- |
| Scheme name | The EDrill Family Bursary |
| Phase 1 geography | Thailand operations only |
| Intended first Award Year | From 1 October 2026, subject to approval |
| Maximum awards | Up to 6 bursaries per Award Year |
| EDrill employee award value | Up to THB 120,000 per awardee per Award Year |
| Labour-hire award value | Up to THB 8,000 per month, paid quarterly, if approved |
| Administration | Bangkok HR, with Finance and Operations support |
| Scheme type | Internal company-funded discretionary bursary |
| Funding entity | Energy Drilling Pte. Ltd. (EDPL) |
| EDrill employee payment route | Normal payroll session to recipient employee bank account, with payslip line item |
| Internal compliance position | Chairman/Committee-approved internal compliance position; no external legal advice obtained |

## 3. Key Controls

The Bursary will not be launched until the following are complete or recorded:

- routine Finance/payroll administration setup, including cost centre / GL code if required;
- applicant consent, confidentiality, data minimisation, anonymised Committee review where practicable, and restricted access controls;
- labour-hire agency acknowledgement, if applicable;
- conflict-of-interest and confidentiality declarations before Committee review/decision activity;
- selection method;
- final internal and external communications wording.
