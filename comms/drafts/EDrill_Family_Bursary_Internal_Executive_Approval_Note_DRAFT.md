# The EDrill Family Bursary - Internal Executive Approval Note

Status: Draft for internal review only  
Version: 0.1  
Purpose: obtain executive approval to proceed with the Phase 1 company-funded Bursary launch, subject to closure of legal, finance, payroll, privacy, and operational gates.

## 1. Decision Requested

Approval is requested to proceed with finalising The EDrill Family Bursary as an internal company-funded discretionary education support scheme for eligible families connected with EDrill Thailand operations.

Approval is also requested to continue preparing the document pack, application process, and communications, subject to final review by Finance/Payroll, Legal/Compliance, HR, and Operations.

## 2. Proposed Launch Scope

| Item | Proposed position |
| --- | --- |
| Scheme name | The EDrill Family Bursary |
| Phase 1 geography | Thailand operations only |
| Intended first Award Year | From 1 October 2026, subject to approval |
| Maximum awards | Up to 6 bursaries per Award Year |
| EDrill employee award value | Up to THB 120,000 per awardee per Award Year |
| Labour-hire award value | Up to THB 8,000 per month, paid quarterly, if approved |
| Administration | Bangkok HR, with Finance and Operations support |
| Scheme type | Internal company-funded discretionary bursary |

## 3. Key Controls

The Bursary will not be launched until the following are confirmed:

- funding entity and cost centre;
- budget approval;
- Thai tax/payroll treatment;
- payment route;
- privacy and consent wording;
- discretionary/non-contractual wording;
- labour-hire agency participation model, if applicable;
- committee membership;
- selection method;
- internal and external communications wording.
