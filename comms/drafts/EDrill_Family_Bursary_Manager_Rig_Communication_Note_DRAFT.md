# The EDrill Family Bursary - Manager / Rig Communication Note

Status: Draft for internal review only  
Version: 0.1  
Audience: managers, supervisors, OIMs, rig leadership, HR, and Operations coordinators.

## 1. Purpose

This note helps managers and rig leaders explain The EDrill Family Bursary consistently.

Managers should not make promises about eligibility, award outcomes, payment timing, renewal, or exceptions. All formal questions should be referred to Bangkok HR.

## 2. Key Message

The EDrill Family Bursary is a company-funded discretionary education support initiative for eligible families connected with EDrill Thailand operations.

It is intended to support approved schooling costs for eligible children, subject to criteria, evidence, available places, and annual review.

## 3. What Managers May Say

Managers may say:

- The Bursary is being introduced for eligible Thailand operations families.
- Bangkok HR will administer the application process.
- Applications must be supported by documents.
- Awards are limited and discretionary.
- Eligibility does not guarantee an award.
- Renewal is not automatic.
- Questions should be directed to Bangkok HR.

## 4. What Managers Must Not Say

Managers must not say:

- that an employee is guaranteed to receive an award;
- that renewal is guaranteed;
- that the Bursary is a contractual benefit;
- that the Bursary is a charity, foundation, registered society, or public fund;
- that donations are being accepted;
- that labour-hire agency employees are EDrill employees;
- that payment is outside approved tax, payroll, withholding, reporting, or accounting processes.

## 5. Labour-Hire Agency Wording

If labour-hire agency participation is approved, use careful wording:

> eligible labour-hire agency employees assigned to EDrill rigs

or:

> eligible contractor personnel assigned to EDrill rigs

Do not describe labour-hire agency personnel as "our employees".

## 6. Questions To Refer To HR

Refer the following to Bangkok HR:

- eligibility questions;
- application evidence questions;
- salary threshold questions;
- payment questions;
- appeal or exception requests;
- privacy or document handling questions;
- labour-hire agency participation questions.
