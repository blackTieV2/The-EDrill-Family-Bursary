# The EDrill Family Bursary - Launch Approval Checklist

Status: live approval checklist  
Purpose: confirm that all required approvals and launch gates are closed before The EDrill Family Bursary is issued, applications open, awards are made, or public communications are published.

## 1. Approval Status Key

- Open: not yet approved.
- Approved: confirmed by accountable owner.
- Conditional: approved subject to stated conditions.
- Not applicable: not required for this launch.

## 2. Hard Rule

The Bursary is not launch-ready until every required item in this checklist is Approved, Conditional with conditions satisfied, or Not applicable.
