Status: Draft approval record
Purpose: record approval to move specified Phase 1 documents from draft folders to approved folders.

This record does not launch the Bursary, open applications, approve awards, or authorise publication. The Bursary is not operational until this record, the 2026-2027 Launch Evidence Register, and the Launch Approval Checklist are completed.

## 1. Approval Scope

This record covers final issue approval for the Phase 1 document pack for The EDrill Family Bursary.

Phase 1 is:

- company-funded by Energy Drilling Pte. Ltd. (EDPL);
- Thailand-only;
- discretionary;
- not fundraising;
- not a charity, society, foundation, public fund, or ROS body;
- not an activation of any Singapore ROS or future fundraising pathway.

No external legal advice was obtained. The scheme proceeds on a Chairman/Committee-approved internal compliance position, using the controls recorded in the approved Phase 1 documents.
