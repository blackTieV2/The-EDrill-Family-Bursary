# The EDrill Family Bursary - Document Register and Implementation Roadmap

Working status: project control document  
Purpose: track the documents, decisions, internal compliance controls, and launch actions required to establish and operationalise The EDrill Family Bursary.
Scope: Phase 1 Thailand company-funded bursary, with Phase 2 future Singapore ROS / employee-member fundraising pathway kept in view.

## 1. Current Project Position

Three foundation documents now exist:

1. `EDrill_Family_Bursary_Setup_Brief.md`
   - Captures the email source facts, assumptions to challenge, core design choices, and proposed document pack.

2. `EDrill_Family_Bursary_Decision_Questionnaire.md`
   - Historical/reference questionnaire. Later Decision Log entries supersede any older open-question wording.

3. `EDrill_Family_Bursary_Legal_Compliance_Action_Note.md`
   - Identifies internal compliance controls, tax/privacy questions, Singapore ROS/fundraising considerations, and evidence file requirements.

Confirmed Phase 1 management decisions have closed or reclassified the earlier funding entity, payroll/payment route, committee appointment, internal privacy/consent, and labour-hire payment model blockers. The Bursary Charter and Eligibility and Selection Rules are approved for controlled issue as version 1.1, effective 1 October 2026. The approved Eligibility and Selection Rules are at `01_drafting/eligibility-criteria/approved/EDrill_Family_Bursary_Eligibility_and_Selection_Rules.md`. Version 1.1 generalises the age rule from 2026 calendar-year wording to Award Year wording. No substantive change to intended maximum age: child may turn 12 during the Award Year but must not turn 13 during that Award Year. Remaining documents stay in draft locations unless separately promoted, and the bursary should not be treated as operational until document approval, launch evidence/admin setup, agency participation execution where applicable, and final communications checks are recorded in the Launch Approval Checklist.

## 2. Working Principle

Phase 1 should be an internal company-funded discretionary education bursary for eligible families connected with EDrill Thailand operations.

Phase 1 should not be presented as:

- a charity;
- a foundation;
- a registered society;
- a public fund;
- a donation-funded programme;
- an employee entitlement.

Phase 2 may later consider a Singapore Registry of Societies or other structure if the initiative grows into a member-funded or employee-funded body.

## 5. Recommended Implementation Roadmap

### Stage 1 - Decision Closure

Objective: record the decisions that control legal form, eligibility, payment, tax, privacy, and governance.

Actions:

1. Use the Decision Log as the current source of truth.
2. Keep HR/Ops/Finance administrative details aligned to the approved Phase 1 positions.
3. Use Finance/Payroll records for routine payroll administration.
4. Use the Chairman/Committee-approved internal compliance position; no external legal advice obtained.
5. Ask each appointed labour-hire agency to review and complete practical acknowledgement if its employees are included.
6. Review `EDrill_Family_Bursary_Launch_Workplan_and_RACI.md`.
7. Establish the launch evidence file using `EDrill_Family_Bursary_Launch_Evidence_File_Register.md`.
8. Record decisions in a single approved decision log.

Output:

- Updated Decision Log.
- Routine Finance / Payroll administration records, if used.
- Chairman/Committee-approved internal compliance position.
- Confirmed document assumptions.
- Completed labour-hire agency acknowledgement, if applicable.
- Approved Launch Workplan and RACI.
- Launch evidence file opened with access controls.

### Stage 2 - Draft Phase 1 Document Pack

Objective: create the operational documents for a controlled first launch.

Actions:

1. Draft Bursary Charter.
2. Draft Procedure / SOP.
3. Draft Eligibility and Selection Rules.
4. Draft Payment and Evidence Rules.
5. Draft Privacy and Records Appendix.
6. Draft Labour-Hire Agency Participation Appendix, if applicable.
7. Draft application, review, award, renewal, and appeal forms.

Output:

- Complete draft document pack ready for review.

### Stage 3 - Review and Approval

Objective: make sure the documents can be issued safely.

Actions:

1. HR reviews operational practicality.
2. Finance reviews payment, evidence, and accounting controls.
3. Chairman/Committee reviews discretion, privacy, labour-hire agency, and communication wording using the approved internal compliance position.
4. Executive Sponsor approves final pack.
5. Store approved versions in the launch evidence file.
6. Confirm all issue versions match the approved decisions and payment route.

Output:

- Approved charter.
- Approved SOP.
- Approved forms and templates.
- Approved launch evidence file.

### Stage 4 - Launch Preparation

Objective: prepare staff, committee, and communications.

Actions:

1. Confirm committee member availability.
2. Issue conflict of interest and confidentiality declarations before review/decision activity.
3. Brief Bangkok HR and Operations.
4. Prepare internal announcement.
5. Prepare employee FAQ.
6. Prepare application window and submission method.
7. Prepare secure records folder and retention controls.

Output:

- Launch-ready administration process.

### Stage 5 - First Award Cycle

Objective: run the first cycle fairly and keep auditable records.

Actions:

1. Open applications.
2. Screen eligibility.
3. Verify evidence.
4. Score applications if applications exceed slots.
5. Record committee decisions and conflicts.
6. Issue award and unsuccessful letters.
7. Process payments according to the approved payroll or labour-hire agency onward-payment route.
8. Track quarterly evidence and payments.

Output:

- First bursary awards issued.
- Payment records.
- Decision records.

### Stage 6 - Annual Review

Objective: improve the scheme and preserve long-term options.

Actions:

1. Prepare annual scheme report.
2. Review uptake, fairness, spend, and operational issues.
3. Review whether award values or eligibility need adjustment.
4. Review whether Myanmar or other locations should be considered.
5. Review whether any employee/member funding interest exists.
6. If funding interest emerges, start Phase 2 Singapore ROS/fundraising feasibility review before accepting contributions.

Output:

- Annual review report.
- Approved changes for next cycle.

## 6. Drafting Rules For The Next Stage

When formal drafting begins, every document should:

- describe the scheme as discretionary;
- avoid the words charity, foundation, public fund, donation, or registered society for Phase 1;
- keep labour-hire agency wording separate and precise;
- state that renewal is not automatic;
- state that payment is subject to continued eligibility and evidence;
- include conflict of interest controls;
- include consent, confidentiality, data minimisation, anonymised committee review where practicable, and restricted access controls;
- preserve the company's right to amend, suspend, or discontinue the scheme;
- preserve the future option to restructure the scheme if it grows.
