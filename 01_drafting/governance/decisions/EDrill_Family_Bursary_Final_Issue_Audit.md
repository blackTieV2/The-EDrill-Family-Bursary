## 3. Findings by Severity

### Critical Blocker

No critical blocker was identified in the audited document pack.

No audited file states that the Bursary is operational, launched, live, finally issued, or ready for use without completing the remaining approval and launch evidence gates.

### Major Correction

No major correction was required.

The audited pack is consistent with the following confirmed decisions:

- Energy Drilling Pte. Ltd. is the funding entity.
- Phase 1 Committee membership is confirmed.
- Approved EDrill employee awards are paid through the normal payroll session into the employee's bank account and shown as a payslip line item.
- Bursary payments are tax liable to the recipient, and the recipient remains responsible for their own tax position.
- Labour-hire wording is agency-neutral and does not describe labour-hire agency employees as EDrill employees.
- A family may apply for multiple eligible children, with each child/application means-tested and assessed separately.
- There is no automatic renewal and no automatic renewal priority.
- Rejected applicants may appeal to the Chairman.
- Core applicant evidence is a birth certificate or approved equivalent and school enrolment letter or confirmation.
- The inaugural year is Thailand-only, with future geographic expansion left for later review.
- No external legal advice was obtained; Phase 1 proceeds on the Chairman/Committee-approved internal compliance position.

### Minor Wording

One minor wording item was corrected:

- `01_drafting/committee-terms-of-reference/draft/EDrill_Family_Bursary_Committee_Review_Sheet_DRAFT.md` previously listed the recommended payment route as "Direct to school / reimbursement / other". This was updated to "EDrill employee payroll-session bank payment / labour-hire agency onward payment / approved exception" to match the confirmed payment model.

The remaining references to `Kobchok` are acceptable because they either appear in the retained file name or explicitly state that the appendix is agency-neutral and is not limited to Kobchok.

### No Issue

The application process is usable and clear: applicant steps, evidence requirements, consent, and appeal routes are stated without requiring payslips as standard applicant evidence.

The Committee review process is usable and clear: eligibility screening, conflict checks, need-weighted assessment, tie-breakers, recommendation recording, and escalation routes are documented.

The payroll instruction process is clear for EDrill employees: Committee approval is followed by Chairman instruction to payroll, with payroll-session payment, bank account transfer, payslip line item, and recipient-taxable treatment.

The labour-hire route is practical and light-touch: EDPL pays the appointed agency, the agency pays onward through its existing payroll/payment process, and EDrill may request confirmation of onward payment without creating a burdensome new process.

Evidence requirements are not excessive: birth certificate or approved equivalent and school enrolment confirmation are the core applicant evidence, while Finance verifies salary eligibility internally.
