# The EDrill Family Bursary - Future Singapore ROS Feasibility Note

Status: Future pathway note  
Purpose: preserve the option to develop The EDrill Family Bursary into a Singapore registered society or related structure if the initiative later grows beyond a company-funded internal scheme.

## 1. Current Position

The Phase 1 Bursary is designed as an internal company-funded discretionary education support scheme for eligible families connected with EDrill Thailand operations.

Phase 1 should not be described as:

- a Singapore registered society;
- a charity;
- a foundation;
- a public fund;
- a donation-funded programme;
- an employee/member-funded scheme.

## 2. When ROS Becomes Relevant

Singapore Registry of Societies consideration becomes relevant if EDrill later wishes to create a separate association, club, or member body of 10 or more persons that is not already registered under another law.

Possible triggers:

- employees or members formally join a Bursary association;
- members vote on office bearers or rules;
- the Bursary has its own committee independent of the company;
- funds are held separately from company accounts;
- the scheme accepts member contributions;
- the scheme seeks a separate identity beyond an internal company programme.

## 3. ROS Registration Considerations

A future society model would likely need:

- society name and alternative names;
- registered address;
- constitution in English;
- President, Secretary, and Treasurer;
- member information;
- committee structure;
- objects/purposes;
- membership rules;
- financial rules;
- audit/account rules;
- amendment rules;
- dissolution rules.

The ROS constitution template should be used as the starting point if the future society route is chosen.

## 4. Key Strategic Question

Before moving to ROS, EDrill should decide whether the future model is actually best served by a society.

Alternative structures may include:

- continued internal company-funded welfare scheme;
- company limited by guarantee;
- registered charity;
- trust;
- employee welfare fund;
- informal internal contribution scheme, if legally permitted;
- partnership with an existing charity or foundation.

The choice affects tax, governance, fundraising, liability, audit, donor confidence, and administrative burden.

## 5. ROS Versus Charity Versus Fundraising

ROS registration and fundraising approval are not the same issue.

A society may still need to comply with charity or fundraising requirements if it asks people to contribute money or property for charitable, benevolent, or philanthropic purposes.

If funds are raised in Singapore for beneficiaries in Thailand or Myanmar, foreign charitable purpose fundraising rules may be relevant and must be checked before any appeal begins.

## 6. Future Readiness Requirements

If the future pathway is pursued, EDrill should prepare:

1. legal structure recommendation;
2. purpose/object clause;
3. membership model;
4. governance model;
5. finance and audit model;
6. fundraising model;
7. beneficiary eligibility rules;
8. data protection model;
9. brand/name permission;
10. dissolution and remaining funds clause.

## 7. Decision Gates Before ROS Work Starts

| Gate | Owner | Required decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Future legal structure | Executive Sponsor / Legal | Society, charity, company, trust, or internal scheme |
| Membership model | Executive Sponsor / HR / Legal | Who may join and vote |
| Funding model | Finance / Legal | Company funds, member contributions, public donations, or mixed |
| Beneficiary geography | Sponsor / HR | Thailand only, Myanmar, Singapore, or wider |
| EDrill name use | Company / Legal | Whether separate body may use the EDrill name |
| Fundraising route | Legal / Compliance | Whether permits, notifications, or charity registration are needed |

## 8. Recommended Position

Do not start accepting employee/member contributions until the legal structure and fundraising obligations are confirmed.

Do not register a society merely because the Bursary "catches on". Register only if a separate member-based body is actually the right structure.
