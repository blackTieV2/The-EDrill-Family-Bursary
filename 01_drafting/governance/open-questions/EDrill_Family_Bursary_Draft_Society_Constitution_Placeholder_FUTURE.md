# The EDrill Family Bursary Society - Draft Constitution Placeholder

Status: Future placeholder only  
Purpose: preserve a possible future Singapore society structure.  
Important: this is not ready for filing with the Registry of Societies. If EDrill decides to create a society, the official ROS constitution template should be used as the starting point and Singapore legal advice should be obtained.

## 1. Name

The name of the Society shall be:

> The EDrill Family Bursary Society

Alternative names may be required if applying under the normal ROS registration process.

Use of "EDrill" must be approved by the relevant EDrill company before any society registration or public use.

## 2. Place of Business

The place of business shall be at:

> [Singapore registered address to be confirmed]

The Society shall carry out its activities only at places and in ways permitted by applicable law and regulatory approvals.

## 3. Objects

The proposed objects may include:

1. To support education-related assistance for eligible children and families connected with approved EDrill operations.
2. To administer bursary funds according to transparent eligibility, selection, and financial controls.
3. To promote responsible support for schooling and family welfare among eligible beneficiaries.
4. To raise, receive, manage, and apply funds only as permitted by applicable law and regulatory approvals.

These objects must be reviewed carefully. If the Society's purposes are charitable, benevolent, philanthropic, mutual aid, or employee welfare in nature, different regulatory consequences may follow.

## 4. Membership

Membership model to be decided.

Possible member categories:

- ordinary members;
- associate members;
- corporate/supporting members;
- honorary members.

Key decisions required:

- Who can join?
- Must members be EDrill employees?
- Can contractors join?
- Can non-employees join?
- Can members vote?
- Are there subscriptions or contributions?
- Can foreign persons be members or office bearers?

## 5. Entrance Fees, Subscriptions, and Contributions

No membership fee, subscription, or contribution model should be included until the funding and fundraising structure is legally confirmed.

If member contributions are introduced, the Society must determine whether such contributions are:

- membership subscriptions;
- donations;
- fundraising proceeds;
- restricted funds;
- unrestricted funds.

## 6. Management Committee

A future society would likely require a committee including at least:

- President;
- Secretary;
- Treasurer.

Other possible roles:

- Vice President;
- Assistant Secretary;
- Assistant Treasurer;
- ordinary committee members;
- bursary selection subcommittee.

Office bearer eligibility, election, term length, removal, and replacement rules must be drafted according to ROS requirements and the chosen governance model.

## 7. Duties of Office Bearers

Placeholder duties:

- President: chairs meetings and provides overall leadership.
- Secretary: keeps membership records, minutes, notices, and filings.
- Treasurer: manages accounts, receipts, payments, budgets, and financial reporting.
- Committee: approves policies, supervises bursary administration, manages risk, and ensures compliance.

## 8. Meetings

To be drafted:

- annual general meeting;
- extraordinary general meeting;
- committee meetings;
- quorum;
- notice periods;
- voting rights;
- minutes;
- conflict of interest procedures.

## 9. Finance and Accounts

To be drafted:

- bank account controls;
- authorised signatories;
- annual accounts;
- audit or independent examination;
- spending approvals;
- restricted fund controls;
- reimbursement controls;
- record retention;
- financial reporting to members and regulators.

If fundraising is conducted, additional accounting and submission obligations may apply.

## 10. Bursary Administration

The Society may adopt bursary rules based on the Phase 1 company-funded documents, adapted for the future legal structure.

Rules should cover:

- beneficiary eligibility;
- application evidence;
- selection method;
- conflicts of interest;
- privacy;
- payment controls;
- appeals;
- annual review;
- reporting.

## 11. Fundraising

No fundraising clause should be activated until the Society has confirmed the applicable legal requirements.

Any appeal for funds in Singapore for charitable, benevolent, or philanthropic purposes may be regulated.

Any appeal for funds in Singapore for beneficiaries outside Singapore may require specific foreign charitable purpose review.

## 12. Prohibitions and Restrictions

To be drafted in line with ROS template and legal advice.

Likely restrictions:

- no unlawful activities;
- no political activities unless permitted;
- no improper personal benefit;
- no use of funds outside approved objects;
- no fundraising except as lawfully approved.

## 13. Amendments to Constitution

To be drafted in line with ROS requirements.

Amendments should require member approval and regulatory filing/approval where required.

## 14. Dissolution

To be drafted with care.

The dissolution clause must address:

- member approval;
- settlement of debts;
- treatment of remaining funds;
- prohibition on distribution to members unless lawful;
- transfer to an approved body or purpose, if required;
- final accounts and filings.

## 15. Open Questions Before Drafting Final Constitution

1. Is a society the correct structure?
2. Who are the members?
3. What voting rights do members have?
4. Will funds come from members, company, public donors, or mixed sources?
5. Are beneficiaries in Thailand, Singapore, Myanmar, or multiple countries?
6. Is the purpose charitable, benevolent, mutual aid, employee welfare, or corporate social responsibility?
7. Will charity registration be required or desirable?
8. Will fundraising permits or disclosures be required?
9. Who owns and controls the EDrill name?
10. What happens to funds if the society dissolves?
