# The EDrill Family Bursary - Setup Brief and Decision Register

Working status: pre-drafting discussion document  
Prepared for: EDrill internal review  
Purpose: establish the decisions, checks, and document architecture required before drafting the formal bursary documents.

## 1. Working Aim

The EDrill Family Bursary is intended to support the education of eligible children of Energy Drilling employees in Thailand, with a possible extension to eligible labour-hire agency employees working on EDrill rigs. The immediate objective is to create a controlled company-funded bursary scheme for Thailand operations. A longer-term option is to preserve the governance structure so the scheme could later be adapted into a Singapore-registered society or similar membership-funded structure, if employee/member fundraising becomes part of the model.

This document is not the constitution, procedure, or legal instrument. It is the control brief to decide what those documents must say.

## 2. Known Source Facts From Email Chain

The following points are treated as the current source position:

- The scheme is for children of Energy Drilling employees.
- Initial implementation is for Thailand only.
- The intended start date is 1 October 2026.
- Children must be under 12 years old in the school year of award.
- Eligible employees must have a monthly salary below THB 100,000.
- The bursary applies to children studying in either government or private schools, provided other eligibility criteria are met.
- The bursary is intended for schooling in Thailand.
- Eligible Energy Drilling Thailand employees include eligible Thai crew assigned to ED1, ED2, T15, T16, and GHTH, as well as EVE, subject to salary and age criteria.
- The intended Energy Drilling award value is THB 120,000 per awardee per year, paid quarterly.
- The scheme is limited to a maximum of six bursaries per year.
- The scheme is to be administered by the Bangkok office.
- Awards are made yearly.
- Existing recipients may be considered for continuation in later years, subject to eligibility review and available slots.
- Renewal is not automatically guaranteed.
- Marcus Chew later indicated that children of labour-hire agency employees working on EDrill rigs should also be included.
- labour-hire agency employees must have worked continuously for EDrill since 1 January 2025 to qualify.
- The intended labour-hire bursary value is THB 8,000 per month, to differentiate them from EDrill employees.
- Possible future extension to Myanmar may be considered if there are good reasons, but it is not the present implementation scope.

## 3. Core Framing Decision

The first major decision is whether this is:

1. A company-funded employee welfare / education benefit administered internally; or
2. A separate bursary fund with its own constitution and governance; or
3. A future society, foundation, charity, or trust structure capable of receiving member or public donations.

Recommended short-term framing:

The first version should be a company-funded employee family education bursary scheme, governed by an internal charter and procedure. This avoids creating the appearance of a separate legal body before the company has decided whether it wants one.

Recommended long-term safeguard:

The documents should still use clean governance, transparent selection rules, records, financial controls, conflict-of-interest declarations, privacy controls, and annual reporting. Those features will make a later Singapore ROS or fundraising model easier to build without redesigning the scheme from zero.

## 4. Assumptions To Challenge

These assumptions should not be accepted without testing:

- That a "constitution" is the right first document. It may be better to use "Charter" for the company-funded version and reserve "Constitution" for a later registered society.
- That cash payment to employees is the safest payment method. Direct school payment or reimbursement against receipts may reduce misuse, tax, and perception risk.
- That salary below THB 100,000 is enough to determine need. Household income, number of dependants, and school costs may also matter.
- That "under 12" is clear. The reference date must be defined.
- That private schools should be included without caps. International or high-fee schools could undermine the welfare logic.
- That six bursaries can be selected fairly without an objective scoring method.
- That labour-hire agency workers can be included without creating contractor, vendor, or co-employment sensitivities.
- That the LinkedIn post should be external at launch. Public communication may create expectations before the scheme is fully governed.
- That the same document pack can serve both Thailand implementation and future Singapore ROS registration. It can be designed for compatibility, but the legal form may differ.

## 5. Legal, Tax, and Compliance Checks Required

Before launch, EDrill should obtain local confirmation on the following:

### Thailand

- Whether payments are taxable income or taxable benefits to the employee.
- Whether payments should be processed through payroll, accounts payable, or direct school/vendor payment.
- Whether payment to a child or school changes the tax/accounting treatment.
- Whether the bursary is deductible to the company and under what evidence requirements.
- Whether personal data relating to children, school records, income, and family circumstances requires specific consent or controls under Thai data protection law.
- Whether including labour-hire agency employees creates employment, contractor, vendor-benefit, or anti-bribery concerns.
- Whether any work permit, labour, or social welfare rules are triggered.

### Singapore Long-Term Option

- Whether a future employee/member-funded model would require registration under the Registry of Societies, charity registration, fundraising notification, permit, or another legal structure.
- Whether the use of "EDrill" in the name would require company permission and brand controls if the scheme becomes separate.
- Whether donations from employees are private fundraising, public fundraising, or internal contributions.
- Whether the purpose is charitable, benevolent, employee welfare, or mutual aid, as these labels may change regulatory treatment.

## 6. Decisions Required Before Drafting

The following decisions should be answered before formal documents are drafted.

### Authority and Ownership

1. Which legal entity funds the bursary?
2. Who is the scheme sponsor: Executive Chairman, Thailand Country Manager, HR, or another role?
3. Who has final approval authority for awards?
4. Is the Bangkok office only the administrator, or also the decision authority?

### Eligibility

5. Must the employee be Thai, Thailand-based, assigned to Thailand operations, or employed by a Thailand entity?
6. Is there a minimum service period for EDrill employees?
7. Should the salary threshold be base salary only, or include allowances and regular overtime?
8. Does the THB 100,000 salary threshold apply at application date, award date, or throughout the award year?
9. How is "under 12" measured: on application closing date, start of school year, 1 October, or the child's birthday during the award year?
10. Are adopted children, stepchildren, legal dependants, and children under guardianship included?
11. Is there a maximum number of children per employee?
12. Are children already receiving another company education benefit eligible?

### Schooling

13. Are international schools included?
14. Are homeschooling, special education, online schooling, or vocational/bridging programmes included?
15. What school evidence is required: enrolment letter, fee invoice, receipt, student ID, or attendance record?

### Award Value and Payment

16. Confirm EDrill award value: THB 120,000 per awardee per year, paid THB 30,000 quarterly.
17. Confirm labour-hire award value: THB 8,000 per month, or THB 96,000 per year paid quarterly.
18. Should payment be made to the school, reimbursed to the employee, or paid as cash support?
19. What expenses are eligible: tuition, books, uniforms, transport, meals, technology, exam fees?
20. What happens if school fees are lower than the bursary value?
21. Can unused funds be carried forward?

### Selection

22. If more than six eligible applications are received, how are awardees selected?
23. Should previous recipients receive priority, equal treatment, or lower priority?
24. Should selection be needs-based, lottery-based, merit-based, service-based, or committee-scored?
25. Should applications be anonymised before committee review?
26. Is there an appeal process?

### Labour-Hire Agency Inclusion

27. Who verifies labour-hire agency employment and continuous service since 1 January 2025?
28. Does each labour-hire agency need to acknowledge or co-administer?
29. Is the labour-hire agency benefit paid by EDrill directly, via the labour-hire agency, or to schools?
30. How will EDrill avoid implying that labour-hire agency employees are EDrill employees?

### Governance and Records

31. Who sits on the bursary committee?
32. Who is excluded from decisions where they have a conflict of interest?
33. What records are kept, for how long, and by whom?
34. What annual report is prepared?
35. Who reviews the scheme each year?

### Communication

36. Is the first communication internal only, or also public on LinkedIn?
37. Should the LinkedIn post mention exact values and eligibility, or only the purpose and launch?
38. Should any labour-hire agency be mentioned publicly?
39. Should the scheme be positioned as employee welfare, CSR, education support, or family support?

## 7. Recommended Document Pack

### Phase 1 - Thailand Company-Funded Scheme

These are the recommended first-launch documents:

1. Bursary Charter
   - Purpose, scope, authority, values, governance, annual review.

2. Bursary Procedure / SOP
   - Step-by-step administration from announcement to payment and renewal.

3. Eligibility and Selection Rules
   - Detailed rules, definitions, ranking/scoring, exclusions, tie-breakers.

4. Application Form
   - Employee details, child details, school details, salary declaration, dependants, requested support.

5. Applicant Declaration and Consent Form
   - Accuracy declaration, privacy consent, school verification consent, conflict declaration.

6. Required Evidence Checklist
   - Employment, salary, child relationship, age, school enrolment, fee invoice, bank/payment evidence.

7. Committee Review Sheet
   - Scoring, eligibility check, conflict record, decision record.

8. Award Letter
   - Amount, payment schedule, conditions, renewal not guaranteed, evidence requirements.

9. Unsuccessful Application Letter
   - Polite notification, no entitlement language, future eligibility.

10. Renewal Review Form
    - Continuing eligibility, school progress/enrolment, payment record, annual committee decision.

11. Appeals / Exception Request Form
    - Narrow process for procedural errors or exceptional circumstances.

12. Annual Scheme Report Template
    - Number of applicants, number of awards, total spend, demographic summary, issues, recommendations.

13. LinkedIn / Public Announcement Draft
    - To be approved only after governance and legal checks are complete.

### Phase 2 - Future Singapore ROS / Fundraising Readiness

These should not govern the first version unless EDrill decides to establish a separate body. However, the Phase 1 documents should be compatible with later creation of:

1. Society Constitution
2. Membership Rules
3. Committee Election Rules
4. Donation and Fundraising Policy
5. Financial Controls Policy
6. Conflict of Interest Policy
7. Public Fundraising Disclosure Pack
8. Donor Communication Pack
9. Annual General Meeting Template
10. Dissolution / Transfer of Remaining Funds Clause

## 8. Recommended Governance Model For Phase 1

Suggested committee:

- Scheme Sponsor: senior executive or delegated senior manager
- Chair: Thailand HR or Country Manager
- Administrator: Bangkok HR representative
- Finance Representative: payment, tax, and evidence control
- Operations Representative: verifies rig assignment and employment eligibility
- Optional Non-Voting Reviewer: compliance or senior HR

Suggested minimum controls:

- At least three reviewers for award decisions.
- Finance cannot be sole approver.
- Any reviewer with a related applicant must recuse themselves.
- Applications should be assessed against written criteria.
- Decisions should be recorded with reasons.
- Personal data should be held only by authorised HR/Finance personnel.
- Annual review should confirm eligibility rules, budget, outcomes, and lessons learned.

## 9. Suggested Selection Approach

If there are more eligible applicants than available bursary slots, a transparent scoring model is preferable to management discretion.

Possible scoring categories:

- Employee salary band below THB 100,000.
- Number of dependent children.
- Single-income household or special hardship circumstances.
- School cost burden relative to salary.
- Length of service.
- Prior receipt of bursary.
- Completeness and reliability of evidence.

Potential challenge:

A merit-based model using the child's academic performance may look attractive, but it may conflict with the welfare purpose. If the bursary is intended to support families with education costs, financial need and eligibility should likely matter more than grades.

## 10. Recommended Communication Position

Internal message:

The scheme should be presented as a family education support initiative for eligible employees and eligible workers supporting EDrill operations in Thailand.

External LinkedIn message:

Public announcement should be modest and values-led. Avoid implying public charity status, guaranteed entitlement, or fundraising unless those structures are legally approved.

Suggested public tone:

"Energy Drilling is pleased to introduce the EDrill Family Bursary for eligible families connected with our Thailand operations. The initiative is designed to support access to schooling and recognise the contribution of the people whose work supports our rigs and operations."

Avoid at this stage:

- "Charity"
- "Foundation"
- "Registered"
- "Public fund"
- "Donations welcome"
- "Guaranteed annual support"
- Any implication that labour-hire agency employees are EDrill employees
