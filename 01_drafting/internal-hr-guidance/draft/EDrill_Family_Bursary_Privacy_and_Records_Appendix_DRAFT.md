# The EDrill Family Bursary - Privacy and Records Appendix

Status: Draft for internal review only  
Version: 0.1  
Effective date: To be confirmed  
Owner: To be confirmed  
Important: this draft is a control appendix, not legal advice. The Phase 1 privacy and records approach is a Chairman/Committee-approved internal compliance position; no external legal advice obtained.

## 1. Purpose

This Appendix defines the privacy and records-control principles for The EDrill Family Bursary.

The Bursary requires handling personal data about employees, children, family relationships, schools, salary eligibility, education costs, and possibly hardship circumstances. This data must be collected and used carefully.

## 2. Status of This Appendix

This Appendix is a draft internal control document.

It is approved internally for Phase 1 operation, subject to the approved controls in this document:

- privacy notice wording;
- consent wording;
- consent-based collection and verification;
- confidentiality;
- data minimisation;
- anonymised Committee review where practicable;
- restricted access controls;
- beneficiary publicity consent requirements.

## 3. Data Minimisation

HR and the Committee should collect only information needed to:

- assess eligibility;
- verify evidence;
- rank or score applications;
- approve awards;
- process payment;
- monitor continued eligibility;
- report on the scheme;
- meet audit, legal, tax, payroll, or compliance requirements.

Do not collect sensitive hardship details unless those details are part of the approved scoring method and privacy wording allows collection.

## 4. Provisional Data Categories

The Bursary may require the following data categories:

| Data category | Examples | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Employee identity | name, employee number, department, contact details | application and communication |
| Employment data | role, rig/office, assignment, service period | eligibility |
| Salary eligibility | salary band or threshold confirmation | eligibility and selection |
| Child identity | name, date of birth, identity document | age and identity verification |
| Relationship data | birth certificate, adoption record, guardianship evidence | eligible child verification |
| School data | school name, enrolment, grade/year | schooling eligibility |
| Expense data | invoice, fee note, receipt | payment and reimbursement |
| Family/dependant data | number of dependants, approved hardship data | selection scoring, if approved |
| labour-hire agency data | employment verification, assignment continuity | labour-hire agency eligibility, if applicable |
| Payment data | bank or school payment details | payment processing |

## 5. Data That Should Usually Be Avoided

Unless the Committee approves a specific need, the Bursary should avoid collecting:

- full medical records;
- unnecessary family financial records;
- unrelated identity documents;
- photographs of children;
- detailed school performance records;
- religious, political, or unrelated sensitive data;
- beneficiary stories for publicity.

Where hardship evidence is needed, request the least intrusive evidence that can reasonably support the decision.

## 6. Privacy Notice and Consent

The application pack should include an approved privacy notice and applicant declaration.

The privacy notice should explain:

- what data is collected;
- why it is collected;
- who will access it;
- whether it may be verified with schools, the relevant labour-hire agency, or internal departments;
- whether data may be shared with Singapore management or other group functions;
- how long data will be retained;
- how data will be protected;
- who to contact with questions.

Consent wording should cover, where legally appropriate:

- collection and use of applicant and child data;
- verification of school enrolment and fees;
- internal verification of salary and employment eligibility;
- labour-hire agency verification, if applicable;
- processing and payment administration;
- limited internal reporting;
- retention for audit and compliance.

## 7. Child Data

Child data should be treated with particular care.

Controls:

- collect only what is necessary;
- restrict access to authorised personnel;
- avoid circulating child identity documents by email where possible;
- do not include child names in committee packs unless necessary;
- use application reference numbers where practical;
- do not publish child names, photographs, or stories without specific written consent.

## 8. Access Controls

Access should be limited to:

- Bangkok HR / administrator;
- Finance / Payroll personnel handling payment or tax treatment;
- Operations personnel verifying assignment;
- Bursary Committee members, limited to the data needed for review;
- Executive Sponsor or approving authority.

Finance/HR receives raw applications. Finance verifies salary eligibility internally. Committee packs should be anonymised where practicable and should avoid unnecessary payroll detail. Salary bands or threshold confirmation should be used where possible.

## 9. Verification

Verification may include:

- HR confirmation of employment and service;
- Finance confirmation of salary eligibility;
- Operations confirmation of rig assignment;
- school confirmation of enrolment or invoices;
- labour-hire agency confirmation of employment and assignment, if applicable.

Verification with schools or external parties should occur only where the applicant has provided consent and verification is needed for administration.

## 10. Cross-Border Sharing

If applicant data is reviewed by Singapore management, group HR, or other personnel outside Thailand, use minimised or anonymised data where practicable under the Chairman/Committee-approved internal compliance position.

Until confirmed, use minimised data where possible:

- application reference number;
- eligibility result;
- score/rank;
- salary band rather than salary amount;
- award recommendation;
- no child identity document unless necessary.

## 11. Storage

Bursary records should be stored in a secure location approved by HR/IT or the Committee.

Minimum controls:

- restricted access;
- no public/shared folders;
- no personal email storage;
- no unnecessary printed copies;
- clear folder structure;
- version control for approved documents;
- separation of identity evidence from committee scoring packs where practical.

## 12. Retention

Records should be retained for HR, payroll, audit, tax, and administration needs under restricted access. The Committee may set or update the retention period administratively.

The retention schedule should distinguish:

- successful applications;
- unsuccessful applications;
- withdrawn applications;
- payment records;
- tax/payroll records;
- committee decision records;
- annual reports;
- consent forms.

## 13. Publication and Communications

No beneficiary name, photograph, school name, personal story, or family circumstance should be used in internal or external publicity unless:

- specific written consent has been obtained;
- the consent identifies the intended communication channel;
- the Committee and Communications approve the wording;
- the beneficiary is not pressured to agree.

LinkedIn or external posts should be values-led and should not identify individual beneficiaries unless separately approved.

## 14. Incident Handling

Any suspected loss, misdirected email, unauthorised access, or improper disclosure of Bursary data must be reported immediately to HR and the Committee.

The incident record should include:

- date discovered;
- data involved;
- persons affected;
- how it occurred;
- containment action;
- notification decision;
- corrective action.

## 15. Annual Privacy Review

At the end of each Award Year, HR and the Committee should review:

- whether data collected was necessary;
- whether any data can be removed from future forms;
- whether access controls worked;
- whether any incidents occurred;
- whether retention rules were followed;
- whether communications used beneficiary data appropriately.
