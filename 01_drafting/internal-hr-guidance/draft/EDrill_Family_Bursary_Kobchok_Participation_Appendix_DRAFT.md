# The EDrill Family Bursary - Labour-Hire Agency Participation Appendix

Status: Draft for internal review only  
Version: 0.1  
Important: this appendix must not be used until EDrill Legal/Compliance, Operations, Finance, and the relevant labour-hire agency have confirmed the participation model.

## 1. Purpose

This Appendix defines how eligible employees of the appointed labour-hire agency at the time of application assigned to EDrill rigs may be considered for The EDrill Family Bursary, if labour-hire participation is approved.

The purpose is to support eligible children of workers contributing to EDrill rig operations while preserving the correct relationship between EDrill, each labour-hire agency, and the agency's employees.

## 2. Core Relationship Principle

Labour-hire agency employees are not to be described as EDrill employees unless that is legally accurate.

Approved wording:

> eligible employees of the appointed labour-hire agency at the time of application assigned to EDrill rigs

or:

> eligible employees of EDrill's contractor working on EDrill rigs

Avoid:

- "our employees" when referring to labour-hire agency staff;
- wording that implies employment by EDrill;
- public wording that blurs the contractor relationship.

## 3. Provisional Eligibility

A labour-hire agency employee may be considered if:

1. The relevant labour-hire agency confirms the individual is its employee.
2. EDrill Operations confirms the individual is assigned to EDrill rigs.
3. EDrill Operations and/or the relevant labour-hire agency confirms continuous work for EDrill since 1 January 2025.
4. The child meets age, relationship, schooling, and evidence rules.
5. Required consents and evidence are provided.
6. Finance approves the payment route.
7. Legal/Compliance approves the participation wording and controls.

References to agency records in this Appendix mean the relevant labour-hire agency records.

## 4. Award Value

The provisional labour-hire award value is up to THB 8,000 per month.

The provisional payment cadence is quarterly, up to THB 24,000 per quarter.

Finance and the Executive Sponsor must approve the final amount, cadence, cost centre, tax/payroll treatment, and payment route before awards are issued.

## 5. Verification Requirements

| Requirement | Verifier | Evidence / record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Labour-hire agency employment status | Relevant labour-hire agency | Written confirmation |
| Assignment to EDrill rig | EDrill Operations | Assignment record or written confirmation |
| Continuous work since 1 Jan 2025 | EDrill Operations / labour-hire agency | Service or assignment confirmation |
| Child eligibility | HR | Application evidence |
| School eligibility and fees | HR / Finance | School evidence |
| Payment route | Finance | Approved payment record |

## 6. Labour-Hire Agency Acknowledgement

Before including labour-hire agency employees, EDrill should obtain written acknowledgement from the relevant agency confirming:

- the agency is aware of the Bursary;
- the agency will verify employment status where requested;
- the agency will support assignment/service verification where appropriate;
- the agency will receive any approved bursary payment only for onward payment to the relevant employee, where that route is approved;
- the agency will provide onward-payment confirmation if required by Finance;
- participation does not alter the employment relationship;
- EDrill's support does not make agency employees EDrill employees;
- any data shared will be handled according to approved privacy requirements.

## 7. Payment Controls

Approved working payment route:

1. EDrill pays the relevant labour-hire agency quarterly.
2. The labour-hire agency pays the eligible employee.
3. The labour-hire agency provides onward-payment confirmation if required by Finance.

This route must be approved by Finance and Legal/Compliance before use. EDrill should not make direct payment to a labour-hire employee unless Finance and Legal/Compliance approve that route separately.

## 8. Records and Reporting

Labour-hire applications and awards should be identified separately in internal records and annual reporting.

Records should include:

- labour-hire agency employment verification;
- EDrill assignment verification;
- continuous work verification;
- application evidence;
- award approval;
- payment evidence;
- agency onward-payment confirmation, if required;
- any labour-hire agency acknowledgement.

## 9. Communications

Internal and external communications should refer to labour-hire agency personnel carefully.

Suggested internal wording:

> The Bursary may also include eligible employees of the appointed labour-hire agency at the time of application assigned to EDrill rigs, subject to the approved labour-hire participation rules and verification requirements.

Suggested external wording, if a labour-hire agency is mentioned at all:

> The initiative supports eligible families connected with our Thailand operations, including approved contractor personnel assigned to EDrill rigs.

Do not publicly name any labour-hire agency unless EDrill and that agency have approved the wording.
