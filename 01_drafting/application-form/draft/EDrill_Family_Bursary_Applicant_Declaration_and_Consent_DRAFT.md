## 2. Accuracy Declaration

I declare that:

- the information I have provided is true, complete, and accurate;
- the documents I have provided are genuine and complete;
- I have not intentionally withheld information relevant to eligibility;
- I will notify HR if my circumstances change after submission;
- I understand that false, misleading, or incomplete information may result in rejection, withdrawal of award, suspension of payment, or recovery of payment.

## 3. Understanding of Bursary Conditions

I understand that:

- the Bursary is discretionary;
- eligibility does not guarantee an award;
- the number of awards is limited;
- renewal is not automatic;
- payment is subject to continued eligibility, evidence, and Finance approval;
- the Company may amend, suspend, or discontinue the Bursary subject to applicable law and approval requirements.

## 4. Consent to Collection and Use of Data

Subject to the approved privacy notice, I consent to the collection and use of my personal data and my child's personal data for the purpose of administering The EDrill Family Bursary, including:

- eligibility assessment;
- document verification;
- school enrolment and fee verification;
- salary threshold confirmation;
- employment or assignment verification;
- labour-hire agency verification, if applicable;
- committee review;
- award approval;
- payment processing;
- audit, tax, payroll, and internal compliance purposes;
- annual reporting using minimised or aggregated data where practical.

## 5. Consent to Verification

Subject to applicable law and approved privacy wording, I authorise HR, Finance, Operations, Committee members, and other authorised personnel to verify information relevant to my application with:

- internal HR records;
- Finance or Payroll records;
- Operations assignment records;
- the school identified in my application;
- relevant labour-hire agency or contractor representatives, if applicable.

## 6. Consent to Limited Internal Sharing

I understand that my application information may be shared with authorised personnel involved in administering, reviewing, approving, paying, auditing, or reporting on the Bursary.

Finance/HR receives raw applications. Finance verifies salary eligibility internally. Where practicable, Committee review should use anonymised or minimised information, such as application reference numbers and salary bands, rather than unnecessary detailed personal data.

## 7. Publicity Consent

This declaration does not give permission to use my name, my child's name, photographs, school name, personal story, or family circumstances in publicity.

Separate written consent is required for any internal or external publicity identifying me, my child, or my family circumstances.
