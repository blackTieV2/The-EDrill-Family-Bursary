# EDrill Family Bursary - Marcus Clarification Question

Status: Superseded / no longer outstanding  
Date updated: 11 June 2026

---

## 1. Current Position

No further clarification question for Marcus is currently required before the drafting pack moves forward.

The earlier question on whether awards should be given to the "most needy" eligible applicants first has been superseded by the current working position:

> Applications should be assessed by the Committee using a means-tested, need-weighted approach. A family may apply for multiple children, and each child/application should be assessed under the approved criteria and the annual cap.

---

## 2. Selection Position To Carry Forward

If eligible applications exceed available bursary places, the Committee should rank applications using the approved scoring model, weighted toward financial need and family circumstances.

The scheme should remain a welfare-oriented education support bursary, not an academic prize and not a random draw.

---

## 3. Related Rules Now Reflected Elsewhere

The following user decisions have been incorporated into the working pack:

- Bursary payments are tax liable to the recipient.
- If employment or assignment ends mid-year, the Committee decides case by case based on the circumstances of cessation and the needs of the family.
- Labour-hire wording should refer to the appointed labour-hire agency at the time of application.
- A family may apply for multiple children; all applications are means-tested.
- Core applicant documents are birth certificate or approved equivalent and school enrolment letter or confirmation.
- Finance verifies salary eligibility internally.
- Appeals go to the Chairman of the Committee for review.
- The inaugural year remains Thailand-only, while preserving future expansion flexibility.
