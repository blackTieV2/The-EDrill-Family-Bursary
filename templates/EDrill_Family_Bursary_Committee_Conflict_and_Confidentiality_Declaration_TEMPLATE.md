## 2. Confidentiality Undertaking

I understand that Bursary applications may include confidential employee, child, family, school, salary eligibility, hardship, and payment information.

I undertake to:

- keep all Bursary information confidential;
- use the information only for Bursary administration, review, and decision purposes;
- access only the information needed for my role;
- not copy, download, forward, photograph, or retain application materials outside the approved records process;
- return, delete, or securely dispose of review materials when asked by HR or the Committee Chair.

## 3. Child and Family Data Handling

I understand that child and family data must be handled with particular care.

I agree that:

- child names, identity documents, school details, family circumstances, and hardship information must not be discussed outside the approved Bursary process;
- Committee discussion should use application references and minimised information where practicable;
- raw salary data, child identity documents, and detailed family records should not be circulated unless needed for the approved review process;
- no child name, photograph, school name, story, or family circumstance may be used in communications without specific written consent and the required pre-publication approval.

## 5. Recusal Obligation

I understand that I must:

- notify the Committee Chair promptly if a conflict arises or becomes known;
- recuse myself from reviewing, discussing, scoring, or deciding any affected application unless the Committee Chair records a different approved approach;
- keep the conflicted matter confidential.
