# The EDrill Family Bursary

## Purpose

This repository is the working space for the EDrill Family Bursary program in Thailand. It is used to develop, review, and maintain the documents, templates, records, and communications that support the program from drafting through ongoing operations.

## Program Overview

The EDrill Family Bursary is a corporate welfare initiative funded by Energy Drilling Pte. Ltd. (EDPL) that provides education bursaries to children of eligible employees and eligible labour-hire agency employees assigned to EDrill rigs in Thailand. The Phase 1 Bursary Committee is chaired by Alex Maroske and includes Jacquline Ho, Malee Pasutharnchart, Luke Byrne, and Sittikorn Intahkaew. The planned launch date is 1 October 2026.

Phase 1 uses a Chairman/Committee-approved internal compliance position; no external legal advice obtained. The Bursary Charter and Eligibility and Selection Rules are approved for controlled issue as version 1.1, effective 1 October 2026. The approved Eligibility and Selection Rules are at `01_drafting/eligibility-criteria/approved/EDrill_Family_Bursary_Eligibility_and_Selection_Rules.md`. Version 1.1 generalises the age rule from 2026 calendar-year wording to Award Year wording. No substantive change to intended maximum age: child may turn 12 during the Award Year but must not turn 13 during that Award Year. Remaining documents remain in draft folders unless separately promoted, and operational launch remains subject to the Launch Approval Checklist, Launch Evidence Register, and final communications checks.

## Repository Use

This repo is intended to:

- draft the founding policy and supporting documents;
- store templates and reusable working files;
- track decisions, controls, remaining approval checks, and governance history;
- prepare external communications for LinkedIn and the website;
- manage annual award-cycle records after launch.
